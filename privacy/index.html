Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026 · Applies to the GracePath app for Android and iOS

The short version

  • There is no account and no cloud profile. Your name, streak, saved verses, moods, journal and reminders stay on your phone.
  • When you use the AI features, your words are relayed through our server to Google Gemini and are not stored there.
  • The prayer wall is the one place you write something that other people can see — and only under the first name you choose.
  • GracePath shows no advertising and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not sell data.

What stays on your device

GracePath keeps the following in the app's local storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us:

  • the first name you choose to be called, if you enter one;
  • your faith streak, active days, and mood check-ins;
  • saved verses and reminder times for morning, midday and night prayers;
  • your Spiritual Reflection Journal entries (Guardian Patrons);
  • remembered conversations with your messenger (Guardian Patrons);
  • your patron status and any active Grace Pass;
  • how many messages you have sent today, and whether your one voice sample has been used;
  • prayers you wrote on the wall while the app was offline.

Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling GracePath, removes all of it. There is nothing for us to delete on a server because none of the above is stored there.

What our server receives

Some features need a server: AI conversation, verse reflections, the prayer wall, and the daily message allowance. To keep those working, GracePath sends a random device identifier created on first launch — it is not linked to your name, email, phone number or advertising ID. Our server stores:

What Why
Device identifier, first seen and last seen dates To apply the seven free messages per day without an account
Messages used today, patron status, Grace Pass expiry To serve the right daily allowance
Your prayer wall posts and the prayers and candles they receive To show the wall to everyone
An Expo push token, if you allow notifications To tell you, once, when someone lights a candle on your prayer

Your chat messages, mood verses, verse reflections and journal drafts are processed to produce a reply and are not retained by our server after the response is returned. Please do not send information you would not want processed by an AI provider; there is no need to share your full name, address, or financial details in a conversation.

Google Gemini

The AI features are powered by Google Gemini. Text you send for a conversation, a mood verse, a verse reflection or a journal entry, together with a short persona instruction, is sent from our server to Google for processing. Live voice sessions send your microphone audio to Google only while a session is running; our server issues a short-lived token for that session and does not record the audio. Google's handling of that data is described in the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy.

The prayer wall

When you place a prayer on the wall it becomes visible to other people using GracePath, under the first name you choose (or "A friend" if you leave it blank). Prayers, the number of people praying, and any candle lit on them are stored on our server. The device identifier is kept alongside a post so you can see which prayers are yours and so we can act on reports, but it is never shown on the wall.

The wall is moderated gently. Prayers reported by three people are hidden from public view, and we remove abuse, links and advertising. Hidden prayers are deleted from our server after 14 days. The wall holds at most the 200 most recent prayers.

Purchases and candles

Guardian Patron subscriptions and golden candles are handled by the App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. We never see or store your card details. For a candle, the store gives us a purchase receipt so we can confirm the gift and set the candle glowing for 24 hours; for a subscription, we receive your entitlement status. RevenueCat processes purchase data under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled by your phone, not by our server. If you post on the prayer wall and allow notifications, the app registers an Expo push token with our server so you can receive a single quiet notice when someone lights a candle on your prayer. The token disappears when you stop using the wall or clear the app's data.

Children

GracePath is intended for people aged 13 and over, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Everything on your device is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall GracePath. The Privacy & data panel in the app explains this too.
  • To remove your prayer wall posts, the server record tied to your device identifier, or a push token, write to kotiaiworks@gmail.com and we will delete them within 30 days. Without your device identifier we can still remove a prayer you quote to us.
  • You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings at any time; the app will still work without them.

Security and retention

Requests between the app and our server use HTTPS. We keep server records only as long as they are needed for the purposes above — the daily allowance, patron status, and the public wall — and we keep no conversation history.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app on the next update and move the date at the top of this page.

Who we are

GracePath is made by a small independent team at Koti AI Works. For any privacy question or request, write to kotiaiworks@gmail.com — a person reads every message.
